## Summary

<!-- Summarize the week in 1000 characters or less. -->

This was yet another rough week, but it's hard to pinpoint a source. The timing lines up with the same depression that occurred last year, so it's possible the change of seasons are leaving their mark. From the ground floor though, there was a 26 hour work week at SBUX and a 10 hour work week at TR, and I felt pained because of it. My mom noticed I was burning the candle at both ends, to which I replied "I'm also burning the middle." Aside from the physiological concerns, I struggled alot with my station in life. Much of the week was spent contemplating friendship, as I've been toiling away for years by myself in a basement. On one end, it feels like I've been growing the muscles to bring gatherings to life. On the other, I can't imagine enjoying time with any of the people in my life. I scarcely want to include others, so my plans often occur in isolation (in retrospect, this likely can be attributed to my aversion to preparedness.) Much of my contemplations were rants were I described the nature of my imperfect friendships, how plans have always fallen through and I've not surrounded myself with like-minded individuals. I eventually came to terms with an idea that I'd surrounded myself with sycophants or bots while avoiding any contact with people at a higher station, likely from insecurity, sticking myself in the middle of a venn diagram between who I connect with and who I'm praised by, with the latter being painful. The week concluded with my friends dropping out of plans to go camping, giving me a weekend of reflection. One final point of reflection I'd thought about was the increasing scale of Snarky Puppy, Sebastian Lague, Larnell Lewis, and Nathan Fielder's creations. Of all of these, I'd long assumed the art itself was created with little prep-work, but over time I've begun seeing the long prepataration requirement to fulfill these massive projects, a component of practice I'd neglected. I'm hoping to bring this forward as I practice Aja as a much more exceptional debut of my capabilities.
