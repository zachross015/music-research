## Summary

<!-- Summarize the month in 1000 characters or less. -->

October felt like the first actual month of Fall, both physically (crisp 60 degree weather) and emotionally (falling into a depression.) The month began in prep for PorchFest with Turner and JJ, an event which I invited nearly everyone I knew to and then proceeded to get cancelled due to a hurricane. Either from burnout or upset, this drove me to spend an 11 hour day masturbating and playing video games. I'd be remiss if I didn't mention however that my immediate reaction to this was to buy a protein bar (after grazing Sprouts for an hour and deciding ultimately that chocolate was not the way to go) and asking "what's next?" The final two weeks of the month were spent recovering from getting off on the wrong foot. My loaded schedule has made it exceptionally hard to take care of food and sleep, and while the money is nice, not having enough food to keep up with the hectic schedule has left me drained. Beyond the physiological components, most of the month was spent contemplating where to go from here, which I still struggle to answer. I'd asked myself frequently "is this what I really want?" in an attempt to find an easier resolution to the life I desire. But invariably, the response is not that music is the issue, but Starbucks is. Yet I still can't rid of it and meanwhile and unable to take many opportunities due to the commitment. In conjunction, I'd also thought alot about the role of friendship in my life. I'd rejected two offers, a musical and a jam session, this month due to prior commitments with friends and family. The plans fell through that replaced the musical however, and I'd spent alot of time wondering if I'd made the right decision not going with it. This juncture has revealed to me the mindless approach to my relationships, and how I've frequently surrounded myself with sycophants rather than those who might elevate me. The latter I'm more averse to even when it's been proven that I thoroughly enjoy these people more. But regardless, friendship is an issue in my life that needs to be worked with.
