## Summary

<!-- Summarize the week in 1000 characters or less. -->

This week started with a drive back from Macon, where I reflected on my progress as a musician and the lingering savior complex which has infected my friendships. As it was the first week of the quarter, most of the week was spent contemplating what comes next. This was a destabilizing question, and the alotted indulgence of Pinterest (under the guise of visualization) spent me spinning back into compulsive masturbation among other habits. There was a divide in my mental state through this time. On one side, I've felt more confident than ever. Sharing music with the world has lost any financial, fame, and lust-based motives and has been replaced by a genuine curiosity and striving for perfection. I happened upon the realization that any art is a gamble. The recurring fantasy of booking a cabin to record with a select group of people guarantees no outcome, but instead can be motivated by creating an amazing experience, capturing this, and sharing that with the world. Now on the other side, the constant back and forth of SBUX, TR, running, and practice has me skating burnout. While the confidence I have now is splendid, my mind on this drug has no fortitude for evading compulsion, and so I keep falling into bad habits once again. All that said, obsession has become more culled than ever before and it's getting easier to break the cycles.
