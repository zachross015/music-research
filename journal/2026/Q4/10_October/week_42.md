With the camping trip: often it's just like...one thing that needs to be introduced. Like I didn't need to plan, or really prepare, the whole trip. all I had to do was throw everyone in a chat. My anxiety was more about driving ~5 hours in the dark to a place that required 4WD. This place seems much simpler (3 hours with 2WD accessibility.) and it's cheaper. Beyond that, it's more about throwing everyone into a chat and letting it manifest. So shit like this can either happen in one of two ways:
1. Plan the trip in its entirety as an adventure I want to go on and do so in a way that includes others or
2. Throw everyone into a brainstorm together and let the plan come of its own accord
I realize now that the former creates such resistance because it's an art form. 

But then the question becomes: is art better as the former or latter? I feel like generally the latter, but in some circumstances (eg prince or David Bowie) the former

Regardless, it reminds me that often the only thing standing between thought and action is communicating with people, and everything else (especially resistance due to quality) is just...perfectionism
