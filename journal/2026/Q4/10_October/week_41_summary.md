## Summary

<!-- Summarize the week in 1000 characters or less. -->

This week lived on both extremes. I started the week losing my glasses under a rock on Blood Mountain and rushing back home to get to a lesson with Adrian (which got cancelled). Subsequently, I lost my key (in the trash) Monday morning and had to call out sick. In spite of these, I'd committed to returning to deep meditations for the quarter. And so I did. Returning to a habitual hour long meditation every day completely changed how I felt. Life felt calmer, even amidst the impending hurricane, and I was reminded how clear life can be with this daily practice. I'd been set back a bit from my peak, but I feel like I've grown stronger in my dilligence and am tending to this practice much more disciplined than before. This mindfulness made it so when the news of the hurricane canceling porchfest came in, I was able to fight temptation with ease. At its worst, I sat in Sprouts looking at all the chocolate, candy, pastries, etc that I could buy and saw their transitory nature. I then opted for a sparkling water instead. But on Saturday, I'd fell into a hole. I'd hoped to practice for the day, but either the burnout or the dissapointment got to me and instead I spent 11 hours of the day video gaming. Looking back on that day, it's complicated to tell if I gave into resistance or if I genuinely needed that rest.
