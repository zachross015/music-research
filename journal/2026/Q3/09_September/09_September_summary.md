## Summary

<!-- Summarize the month in 1000 characters or less. -->

This month was a great test of character. My transmission died at the onset, leading me to coordinating a repair with Alex from Starbucks. Whereas before this may have thrown me into a financial nightmare, I'd saved up generously (albeit for the credit card payoff) and recovered from this $2850 pain in the ass within days. The weight of financial burdens being lifted off my shoulder (because of TR) has left me feeling more confident than ever before. But the extended loaded has me feeling socially inept, or rather unmotivated. The same could be said with practice, as in both cases right now my main priority has been devoted to survival. Still, I feel like the restrictions have connected me in a new way to my career and my path of music. I'd spent more time literally reading music: sitting at a piano and reading through Bach (however slowly,) or otherwise reading books, listening to music, and finding time to sit still. Motivation for perfection has largely diverged from clicks and views into the utility and generosity I can provide for others. And disciplined practice has become something I make time for in whichever small doses I can find. I'm closing out the month returning to my goal setting procedures, as they always made life feel clearer. In addition, I've been contemplating the nature of moderation and obsession, and the frictions which I create that perpetuate the latter. I'm hoping to clear more of the unintnetional and mindless behaviors out moving forward, and spend more time perfecting music.
