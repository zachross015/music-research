## Summary

<!-- Summarize the week in 1000 characters or less. -->

This week was another on the hedonic treadmill headed straight for burnout. I started the week looking to take on more, More, MORE. Among the many things I'd divided my time betwee, I was also looking to expand my practice. This, of course, overwhelmed me. Because of how overwhelmed I felt, I spent alot of the week chasing the dragon. Overdid caffeine, masturbated frequently to the old stuff, indulged in alot of sugar, and neglected the important things like diet, exercise, and sleep primarily out of a lack of time or energy. This led me to a deep contemplation on the nature of addiction and obsession in my life, realizing there was no permanent solution and that the concept of "moderation" is an ideal that life should be organized around. The lack of goals in these journals has also led my life into disarray, as I haven't been deciding on a frequent basis what needed to be prioritized (and what should be dismissed.) If nothing else, the importance of this week was realizing that I can't do everything I want to do right now and some things have to lose priority or be cut out entirely. Joe's birthday was on Thursday. Nana's and JT's was on Saturday, and we had a pretty great celebration. This was the climax of my caffeine problems though, because that day I had a coffee, a latte, then espresso at dinner. Decided to detox off caffeine for this week and cut out many unnecessary components.
