## Summary

<!-- Summarize the week in 1000 characters or less. -->

It's hard having an objective stance on the week from the position I felt today. Most of the week was spent contemplating the nature of relationships, beauty, and language learning. Even in clarifying my goals, I felt lost. I think with the uplet of my financial issues on the horizon, the next biggest task in my mind has come down to finding love again. But it seems at competition with the kind of life I want to create in music, and so I fought it. When Karla and Sophie walked in the couple times at SBUX, I saw the paralysis that was tantamount to losing a chess game. I felt an urge to hide myself. Then again with Jennifer, we had several conversations throughout the week and the last of which she'd invited me to the Milton Cirque with her later this year. With the overflow of work in my life, I've been struggling to socially recover, and the consequence has led me to isolation once again. Practice has been suffering slightly (only as a discipline, gig practices are fine) and I've grown more confused about my role as a teacher in the language of music. Still, towards the end of the week I realized what "reading music" actually means, and spent a morning reading Bach onto the piano. It's a great realization which has inspired me to transcribe more music, and has put a greater emphasis in my mind on memorization and perfecting a song as something that can be internally motivated rather than done for clicks and views. Still, the conception that I might fall in love again is both the most energizing and most terrifying thing in my life right now, and I feel hopelessly lost in finding my people, and all the meanwhile I wonder if all this work isn't just an exuse to avoid looking for them. It often also feels absurd what ends up being "progress" for me socially, because often times its just messaging someone or saying hello.
