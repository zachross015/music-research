## Summary

<!-- Summarize the week in 1000 characters or less. -->

It was another difficult week full of compulsion, but upon reflection it makes alot of sense. Started out the week with much discomfort. I'd been hooked on Pinterest and YouTube, mindlessly zoned in on home improvement and botany videos. Both Created a desparate urgency to improve my basement and bedroom without affording changes, which left me treading in desire. In the meantime, the AC was still broken. The combination of these left me in heat exhaustion and unable to pour any effort into exercise, meditation, taking care of my dog, or practicing. In spite of this, I still interviewed with Thunder Road and was given the position, supported Tate at his gig, and then helped Momo move. In the depths of all this, I realized generosity is not limited to money or gifts, but instead can be accomplished through acts of service and quality time. By Saturday, I fell ill. I struggled with focus the entire week and up until today, this panicked me. Reading back on everything, it's obvious that the interview and its side effects distorted me, then the heat and its exhaustion likely led to an increasing illness. The combination exhausted my willpower. This left me with nothing for discipline against vices. Although I felt startled here, I trusted myself to return to a state of generosity once the base conditions had improved, and so I spent most my time allowing the mindless wanderings while I tended to the basics of food, water, and rest.
