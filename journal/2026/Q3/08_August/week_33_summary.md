# Week 34

## Summary

The week started out moving Joe from is apt in Kennesaw back to the house. Four of us, living at home again. It was a rough move, but one I felt stoic in. I spent little time falling into the arguments my mom had laid traps for, and instead got to work with packing, moving, then unpacking while the rest of them took care of the apt. It was bittersweet, because I could see how worried Joe was. He's experiencing what I went through three years ago and I have a lot of compassion for him. The rest of the week led up to a vacation with Taylor and friends. Throughout the week, I'd contemplated Taoism and Stoicism frequently. One concept which marinated throughout the week was in making room for growth to be natural, which id interpreted as taking care of my "soil." Because of this, I'd re-energized frequently via meditations (one lasting 45 min,) eating better, and sleeping more. Also making more room in my schedule and routine as a means of creating headroom. The result had me acting more diligent than I'd been in a long while. It left me contemplating the nature of thought and my own addiction, using thinking as a means of procrastination and prioritizing it over genuine joys like socializing. A recurring theme of the week was creating room for the natural, and how habitual moderation lets indulgence feel amazing when it happens. That and secure socialization. The week wrapped up in Ginny Springs, where I'd spent much time in autotelic experiences with my friends. It felt like a true realization of the visions I'd been having, and I'm grateful of having gotten to experience this weekend through new lenses.
