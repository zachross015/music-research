## Summary

<!-- Summarize the quarter in 1000 characters or less. -->

This quarter was a contemplation on growth and moderation. Turning 29 set an alarm off in my head, reminding me of the progress I'd promised I'd make before turning 30. I spent the beginning of this time preparing a road map for how I might move on with my life before then, while falling back into Pinterest Porn as I visualized the future. This future started with a new job at TR. Accepting this job led me to make more big moves: shifting my schedule to accomodate the new load (including a weekend (FINALLY),) moving from self-help as entertainment to fiction and music and movies, and eventually letting go of social media entirely. For that last point, I'd fought with myself alot leading up to the decision. I tried to make it work over the past 3 months, but at some point it hit me that the senseless jealousy of people like Atticus, Frigga Five, Devin, and Drew was worthless. The life they wanted was not the same as mine, and social media has continued to be a crutch for my motivation. While I'd let it go at the beginning of the year, I'm coming back at it with a different resolve: tempering my obsessive tendencies and moving towards more intentional action. The main component of this finding new ways to be social. This has driven me far outside of my comfort zone towards finding new ways to coordinate friends and bring people togther offline. It's also helped my practice by forcing my to reasses the importance of learning a solo or song to perfection without the goal of getting views from it. I'm blessed with students who have helped me see the benefit I can provide others by transcribing and posting stuff online, as well as demonstrating to me how I may focus my own practices towards learning difficult songs and memorizing them so I may have them anywhere. A couple of other noteworthy things:
- Car broke down and had Alex come check it out on the same day
- Recruited a new student for my own lessons studio
- Enjoyed transcribing a Steve Gadd Solo and decided to pursue more of these spacey solos
- Busted out into a great jam session in Taylor's car
- Got slightly into Transcendentalism and Daoism for a time. Both were great insights, but ultimately I think their lifestyles diverge too far from the kinds of achievements I want to experience
