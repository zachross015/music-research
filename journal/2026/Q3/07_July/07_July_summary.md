## Summary

<!-- Summarize the month in 1000 characters or less. -->
July as the start of the next quarter has been painful, but what start to a quarter isn't? I began with turning 29, which led me into a deeper realization that the goal I'd set for myself, to be self-reliant as a musician by 30, was rapidly encroaching. The shock of this pushed me to work towards my next biggest goal: self-reliance. The beginning of this was defined by visualizing a future I could be proud of, meaning job hunting. It was my first real move out of Starbucks. I created a new resume, a cover letter, and applied to three jobs. Meanwhile, I'd also reached out to Danny about shadowing and began self-promoting through Instagram. The culmination of these big moves opened my eyes to a couple trains of perception I'd never seen: 1. the power of visualizing a goal and how I'd successfully used it before to run a half marathon, 2. how botany relates to generosity, 3. preparation as a conduit for spontaneity, and 4. the endless power of entreprenuership and self-promotion. These 3 inspired my meditations throughout the period as I sought deeper connection with generosity and using goal setting to help others and accept myself better. Leaving the month with my first real job interview as a musician and with a much stronger confidence in my direction. The AC is still out in my house so brain fog is prevading, but I am surviving.
