How can soloing be better practiced?
- Slow way the hell down and place each note intentionally
- trade 2, 4, 8, etc and start combining in terms of sentences rather than just notes
- Work over the same loop for an extended period, with constant iteration over feedback 

In general, the philosophy is moving from instant creation/gratification to long term methods which embrace a more professional attitude, dispelling the illusion that the art itself doesn't require dedication
